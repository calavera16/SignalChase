            All measurements & exact results
            
              
            
                
            
                  
                    
            
                      Measurement
                      Unchanged
                      Median subtraction
                      High-pass
                    
            
                              
                  
                
            
              
            
              
            
                Peaks are algorithmic local-maximum candidates, not verified
                heartbeats. Timing shift is the mean absolute shift of matched
                candidates, paired one-to-one within valid segments with a 150
                ms tolerance in either direction. Waveform difference is
                centered normalized RMSE: center each signal, then divide their
                root mean square difference by the comparison signal's standard
                deviation. It is normalized and unitless. A synthetic reference
                measures reference error; original samples measure processing
                change, not physiological accuracy. Neither establishes a
                universal best method.
